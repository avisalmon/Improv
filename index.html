Play by tapping the keys, or use your computer keyboard: A–; for white keys and W E T Y U O P for black keys. Z/X shift the octave. Space plays and stops, and Enter starts recording a new track. A MIDI keyboard with a sustain pedal works too (Chrome or Edge).
